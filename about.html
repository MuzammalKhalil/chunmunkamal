About Me

Hi! I’m Chunmun and I’m a Holistic Health, Wellness and Resilience Coach who is obsessed with an integrated approach to health and wellbeing.

I didn’t become a coach because it sounded appealing. I became a coach because I experienced first what happens when you ignore the signals that a body that is breaking down sends. I also experienced the power of having someone’s undivided attention and support to regain my health, confidence and capacity to overcome life’s hurdles and challenges.

My interest in personal development, psychology, mental wellbeing, and resilience began during an intense 5 -year period of, back‑to‑back midlife changes that led to health issues and eventually, burnout. Along the way, my body kept signalling that I something wasn’t right. Sometimes, I didn't understand the signals. At other times, I ignored them.

My body forced me to slow down and care for myself. Now, as a Health, Wellness and Resilience Coach, I use my lived experience and knowledge to help individuals in midlife have more energy, vitality and capacity for change.

Education

I hold a bachelor’s degree in Physics, a Masters in Social Work, a Masters in Business Administration and Diploma in Digital Learning Design.

My education and professional experiences shape my coaching approach, combining knowledge from different disciplines along with the wisdom of my Indian heritage.
