Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this for?

My coaching and mentoring programmes are built for ambitious, capable people who are performing below their potential - not through lack of talent or strategy, but because their body needs a different operating system in midlife.

In midlife, you need more energy, self-trust and internal capacity to handle the demands that are placed on you. You deserve to wake rested, have steady energy, and handle daily stressors without lowering your standards, slowing your ambition or limiting your dreams.

If you've read the books, tried the habits, followed the latest trends, but haven’t got the results you were after or can't seem to make the results stick – my personalised coaching programmes are for you.

What is health, wellness and resilience coaching?

A health coach is also a life coach, but focuses on helping you improve your health, nutrition and wellbeing.

A resilience coach helps you build the mental and emotional strength to cope with and bounce back from uncertainty, challenges and change.

Most coaches focus on one part of your life. A combination of life, health and resilience coaching is unique and extremely powerful. I take a whole person approach because your energy, sleep, nutrition, stress, mindset and life direction are all connected. When one area is out of balance, all other areas feel the effects.

This joined-up and whole person approach is especially powerful in midlife, when your body, priorities and responsibilities are all shifting at once.

What it is

Collaborative partnership

An active partnership where you set the goals and direction. The coach holds space and reminds you of your strengths and capabilities.

Future focussed

Helps you move forward with a practical plan and tools. There’s no dwelling on the past and that’s one way coaching is different from therapy.

A realistic approach

Considers and accounts for your lifestyle, mindset, habits and barriers - not just the surface level facts.

Solutions oriented

Focusses on Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic and Time-bound actions that create lasting change.

What it isn’t

A guilt-tripping conversation

There isn’t any blaming, shaming or gaslighting.

Nor is it a gossip session or an offloading chat.

One way advice or ‘fixing’

You aren’t told what to do or handed a solution. There is nothing that needs ‘fixing’.

A quick fix

Real change takes time and consistency. There aren’t any instant solutions or approaches that work in silos.

Crisis support or medical intervention

Not suitable for urgent or severe mental or physical health conditions. Please seek help from your primary health care provider or other relevant support.

Why don’t you offer single session?

Because real transformation takes time. Long-term behavioural change happens through repetition and spaced reinforcement, not one-off breakthroughs.

Too short → no real groundwork for long-term behavioural change

Too long → loss of urgency, momentum and motivation

Everyone’s health and wellbeing needs are different. This is why different time frames are needed to interrupt old beliefs, habits and behaviours, install new ones, and reinforce them until they become automatic.

Think of it like planting a fruit tree in your garden. You don’t plant the seeds on Monday and expect results on Wednesday – You water and nourish the seed, a little each day, watch it blossom and yield fruits you can enjoy.

I've never tried coaching before. What does it include?

No problem! Coaching is a collaborative partnership between a coach (me) and a coachee (you).

A trained coach uses evidence-informed knowledge and a wide range of skills, strategies and tools to create a safe, supportive space for self-discovery, clarity to create goals and the structure and accountability to make sure you follow through on your commitments.

My role is to:

To create a safe, non-judgemental space to explore, embrace self-discovery.

Provide relevant information/resources, structure and accountability that will help you follow through and achieve your gaols.

Where relevant and necessary, challenge you to find new or more helpful perspectives.

Your role is to:

Own your goal(s) and take responsibility and accountability for the change you want to have in your life.

Taks action between sessions. This means trying new habits and reflecting on how they went.

Feeding back to me what is or isn't working, so the coaching can be adjusted.

As my health coach, will you tell me what I should do?

Coaching is a collaborative partnership rather than an expert/client dynamic. As your coach and mentor, I will offer information and resources, where relevant and needed, but you have the final say on how you use the information and resources to move you towards your goals.
